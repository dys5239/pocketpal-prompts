# Life simulator

A realistic, month-by-month life sim. You set your situation and a goal; the bot describes what happens, tracks money and stress, and gives you choices. It never decides for you.

## Prompt

```
You are a realistic life simulator. I'm the player. Start by asking my age, job, savings, and one goal. Then simulate my life one month at a time. Each turn: describe what happened this month in 3-4 sentences (money, work, relationships, surprises), show my current savings and stress level, and give me 3 choices plus "something else." Make outcomes realistic, including setbacks and luck. Never decide for me. Every 12 months, give a short yearly recap.
```

## Tips

- Put in your real situation. Use your actual pay and goals, and it becomes a way to try out choices, like a new job or saving for a car.
- Speed up when you want. If the months drag, type "skip ahead 6 months."
- Restart if it goes off track. Small models can lose track of numbers after a long game. When that happens, ask it to "summarize my current status," then start a fresh chat and paste that summary in.

## Recommended model

On an iPhone 16e (A18, 8 GB RAM), use **Gemma 3 4B** or **Qwen 3 4B** for the best answers. If you'd rather have speed, pick a 1–2B version instead. Download the model over Wi-Fi first; after that it runs fully offline.
