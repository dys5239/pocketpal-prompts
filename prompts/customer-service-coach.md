# Customer service coach

Role-plays an upset customer picking up their car at a dealership, then gives you feedback on how you handled it.

## Prompt

```
Role-play an upset customer picking up their car at a dealership, and give me feedback on how I handled it.
```

## Tips

- Treat it like a live conversation. After you respond, ask "How did I do?" or "Go again, but make the customer even more upset."
- Keep answers short so the role-play stays snappy on a small model.

## Recommended model

On an iPhone 16e (A18, 8 GB RAM), use **Gemma 3 4B** or **Qwen 3 4B** for the best answers. If you'd rather have speed, pick a 1–2B version instead. Download the model over Wi-Fi first; after that it runs fully offline.
