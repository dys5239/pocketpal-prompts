# Trivia and quiz partner

Quizzes you on MLB history, one question at a time, and keeps score.

## Prompt

```
Quiz me on MLB history, one question at a time, and keep score.
```

## Tips

- Tell it a team, era, or difficulty if you want a tighter quiz (for example, "Phillies only" or "start easy").
- Small models can get sports facts wrong. Treat close calls as debate fuel, not a rulebook.

## Recommended model

On an iPhone 16e (A18, 8 GB RAM), use **Gemma 3 4B** or **Qwen 3 4B** for the best answers. If you'd rather have speed, pick a 1–2B version instead. Download the model over Wi-Fi first; after that it runs fully offline.
