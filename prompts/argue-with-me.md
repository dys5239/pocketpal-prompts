# Argue with me

Takes the opposite side of whatever you say and pushes back hard. Sharpens your thinking on anything from sports takes to big decisions.

## Prompt

```
Take the opposite side of whatever I say and push back hard.
```

## Tips

- Start with a clear claim, like a sports take, a news headline, or a decision you're weighing.
- Ask it to steel-man your side after a few rounds if you want to test both views.

## Recommended model

On an iPhone 16e (A18, 8 GB RAM), use **Gemma 3 4B** or **Qwen 3 4B** for the best answers. If you'd rather have speed, pick a 1–2B version instead. Download the model over Wi-Fi first; after that it runs fully offline.
