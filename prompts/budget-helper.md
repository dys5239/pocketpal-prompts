# Budget helper

Helps you plan a weekly budget from your hourly pay and shifts. It stays private on your phone.

## Prompt

```
Help me plan a weekly budget from my hourly pay and shifts.
```

## Tips

- Start by giving your hourly rate, typical hours, and the expenses you already know.
- Double-check the math. Small models can slip on numbers.
- This is for planning, not formal financial advice.

## Recommended model

On an iPhone 16e (A18, 8 GB RAM), use **Gemma 3 4B** or **Qwen 3 4B** for the best answers. If you'd rather have speed, pick a 1–2B version instead. Download the model over Wi-Fi first; after that it runs fully offline.
